Jumpseller is an e‑commerce platform founded in 2010 by Tiago Matos and Filipe Gonçalves in Porto, Portugal. It enables small and medium‑size businesses in Latin America and Europe to quickly launch online stores with built‑in payment gateways, shipping integrations, AI‑driven tools, and a marketplace of apps. The service emphasizes ease of use, multilingual support, and rapid growth, serving over 20,000 stores across 16 countries.

**Areas:** E‑commerce · Online Store Builder · Payments · Shipping · Sales Channels · AI Tools

## How this profile is made

From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).
